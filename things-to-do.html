Things to Do

Parks, trails, and landmarks to explore in and around Wallingford.

Parks & trails

Wharton Brook State Park

A state park popular for picnicking, fishing, and short walking trails.

Connecticut DEEP — Wharton Brook

Community Lake Park

Walking paths, playgrounds, sports fields, and a lake in a central town park.

Tyler Mill Preserve

Hundreds of acres of woodland and trails for hiking and mountain biking.

Quinnipiac River Linear Trail

A multi-use trail following the Quinnipiac River for walking and cycling.

Landmarks & attractions

Choate Rosemary Hall

One of the country's best-known independent schools, with a historic campus in the heart of Wallingford.

choate.edu

Toyota Oakdale Theatre

A major concert and event venue at 95 S Turnpike Road, hosting national touring acts.

oakdale.com

Gouveia Vineyards

A family vineyard on Whirlwind Hill Road with tastings and hilltop views.

gouveiavineyards.com

Paradise Hills Vineyard & Winery

A local winery offering tastings and events in a scenic setting.

paradisehillsvineyard.com

Seasonal events

Wallingford hosts recurring community events through the year. Notable ones include:

  • Celebrate Wallingford — an annual autumn festival in downtown Wallingford with food, music, vendors, and family activities. Confirm the date on the town's events calendar.
  • Farmers' markets — seasonal markets with local produce and goods.
  • Concerts and performances — at the Toyota Oakdale Theatre and town green.
