Wallingford at a glance

Founded 1670

One of New Haven County's oldest towns, with deep roots in Connecticut's manufacturing history.

~44,400 residents

Population per the 2020 U.S. Census, spread across about 40 square miles.

Home to Choate

Choate Rosemary Hall, one of the nation's best-known independent schools, calls Wallingford home.
