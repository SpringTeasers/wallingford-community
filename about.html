History

Wallingford was settled in 1670 by families from New Haven, and the town was named for Wallingford, England. It grew as an agricultural community before becoming a center of manufacturing, most notably in silverware and electroplating during the 19th and early 20th centuries. Today Wallingford blends residential neighborhoods, a walkable downtown, and a diverse mix of local businesses.

Government

Wallingford operates under a mayor–council form of government. The mayor serves as the chief executive, and the nine-member Town Council serves as the legislative body. As of 2024, the mayor is Vincent Cervoni.

Residents also receive local services through departments covering public works, parks and recreation, public safety, health, and the town's municipal electric and water divisions.
